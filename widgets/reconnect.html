Grok Bot needs a quick reconnect
Its cloud computer fell asleep, and only the Grok Bot app can wake it now.
  1. 1
    Send any message to a bot in Grok Bot
    That wakes the computer.
  2. 2
    Tap Reconnect
    Grok Bot restarts. An unsent draft in Grok Bot is lost.
Takes about half a minute
